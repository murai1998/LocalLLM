From memory, **12 people** have ever walked on the Moon’s surface. All were American NASA astronauts during the Apollo program, from **Apollo 11 in 1969** through **Apollo 17 in 1972**.

1. **Neil A. Armstrong** — Apollo 11, 1969  
   Armstrong was a former naval aviator and test pilot who piloted the Lunar Module *Eagle* during Apollo 11. He made the first human step onto the lunar surface on July 20, 1969.

2. **Edwin “Buzz” Aldrin** — Apollo 11, 1969  
   Aldrin was an astronautical engineer and test pilot who served as Apollo 11’s lunar module pilot. He was the second person to walk on the Moon and helped collect samples and test equipment during the first lunar landing.

3. **Charles “Pete” Conrad Jr.** — Apollo 12, 1969  
   Conrad was a veteran test pilot who had flown Gemini 5 and Apollo 8 before commanding Apollo 12. He was the third person to walk on the Moon and led a precise landing near the Surveyor 3 lander.

4. **Alan L. Bean** — Apollo 12, 1969  
   Bean was a naval aviator, test pilot, and lunar module pilot who became the fourth person to walk on the Moon. After leaving NASA, he became a well-known artist and author.

5. **Alan B. Shepard Jr.** — Apollo 14, 1971  
   Shepard was the first American in space and a member of the Mercury 7 astronaut group. He commanded Apollo 14 and became the fifth person to walk on the Moon.

6. **Edgar D. Mitchell** — Apollo 14, 1971  
   Mitchell was an engineer and lunar module pilot who became the sixth person to walk on the Moon. After his NASA career, he became known for research and writing on parapsychology and consciousness.

7. **David R. Scott** — Apollo 15, 1971  
   Scott was a test pilot and geology-trained commander who became the seventh person to walk on the Moon. He performed the famous hammer-and-feather demonstration in the Moon’s low gravity.

8. **James B. Irwin Jr.** — Apollo 15, 1971  
   Irwin was a geologist and lunar module pilot who became the eighth person to walk on the Moon. He collected lunar samples and helped operate the first lunar rover.

9. **John W. Young** — Apollo 16, 1972  
   Young was a veteran of Gemini 3, Skylab, and STS-1 who commanded Apollo 16. He was the ninth person to walk on the Moon and helped extend the range of Apollo surface operations.

10. **Charles M. Duke Jr.** — Apollo 16, 1972  
   Duke was a geologist and lunar module pilot who became the tenth person to walk on the Moon. He was the first African American to walk on the Moon and later served as NASA administrator.

11. **Eugene A. Cernan** — Apollo 17, 1972  
   Cernan was a geology-trained test pilot and lunar module pilot who became the 11th person to walk on the Moon. He was the last person to leave the lunar surface, making him the last man on the Moon.

12. **Harrison H. Schmitt** — Apollo 17, 1972  
   Schmitt was a professional geologist and lunar module pilot who was the last of the 12 to step onto the lunar surface. He was the only scientist among the Moonwalkers and led the most geologically focused Apollo landing.

Here are the 12 people who walked on the lunar surface:

**Apollo 11**
1. **Neil Armstrong**: The first human to step onto the moon's surface. He served as the commander of the Apollo 11 mission.
2. **Buzz Aldrin**: The second person to walk on the moon. He was the Lunar Module Pilot for the first landing.

**Apollo 12**
3. **Pete Conrad**: The commander of Apollo 12. He is remembered for his humor and for being the third person to walk on the moon.
4. **Alan Bean**: The Lunar Module Pilot for Apollo 12. After his NASA career, he became a renowned artist specializing in space scenes.

**Apollo 14**
5. **Alan Shepard**: The commander of Apollo 14. He was the first American in space (during the Mercury program) before walking on the moon.
6. **Edgar Mitchell**: The Lunar Module Pilot for Apollo 14. He later became well-known for his interests in consciousness and spirituality.

**Apollo 15**
7. **David Scott**: The commander of Apollo 15. He led the first mission to utilize the Lunar Roving Vehicle.
8. **James Irwin**: The Lunar Module Pilot for Apollo 15. He later left NASA to become a minister.

**Apollo 16**
9. **John Young**: The commander of Apollo 16. He is one of the few astronauts to have flown in both the Apollo program and the Space Shuttle program.
10. **Charles Duke**: The Lunar Module Pilot for Apollo 16. He was a professional geologist and one of the last humans to walk on the moon.

**Apollo 17**
11. **Eugene Cernan**: The commander of Apollo 17. He holds the distinction of being the last human to stand on the lunar surface.
12. **Harrison Schmitt**: The Lunar Module Pilot for Apollo 17. He was the only professional scientist (a geologist) to walk on the moon.

Here is the list of the 12 people who have walked on the surface of the moon, organized by their respective missions:

**Apollo 11 (1969)**
1. **Neil Armstrong**: He was the first human to ever step onto the lunar surface. He was a naval aviator and a skilled test pilot.
2. **Buzz Aldrin**: He became the second person to walk on the moon, shortly after Armstrong. He was an aerospace engineer and a naval aviator.

**Apollo 12 (1969)**
3. **Pete Conrad**: He was the commander of the Apollo 12 mission and the third person to walk on the moon. He was a test pilot with extensive experience in high-altitude flight.
4. **Alan Bean**: He served as the Lunar Module pilot for Apollo 12 and was the fourth person to walk on the moon. He was a geologist by training.

**Apollo 14 (1971)**
5. **Alan Shepard**: He was the first American to travel into space (suborbital) and the fifth person to walk on the moon. He was a naval aviator and a test pilot.
6. **Edgar Mitchell**: He was a psychologist who became the sixth person to walk on the moon. He was the first person to have a PhD in psychology to walk on the moon.

**Apollo 15 (1971)**
7. **David Scott**: He was a geologist who walked on the moon during the Apollo 15 mission. He was the first person to use the Lunar Roving Vehicle extensively.
8. **James Irwin**: He was an engineer and test pilot who became the eighth person to walk on the moon. He participated in the Apollo 15 mission.

**Apollo 16 (1972)**
9. **John Young**: He was a veteran astronaut who commanded the Apollo 16 mission. He was the ninth person to walk on the moon.
10. **Charles Duke**: He served as the Lunar Module pilot for Apollo 16 and was the tenth person to walk on the moon. He was a geologist and a test pilot.

**Apollo 17 (1972)**
11. **Eugene Cernan**: He was the commander of the final Apollo mission to land on the moon. He was the last person to walk on the lunar surface.
12. **Harrison Schmitt**: He was a professional geologist and the only scientist to ever walk on the moon. He accompanied Cernan on the Apollo 17 mission.

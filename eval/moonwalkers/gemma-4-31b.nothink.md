Here are the 12 people who have walked on the lunar surface, listed by mission:

**Apollo 11**
1. **Neil Armstrong**: The mission commander and the first human to ever step onto the moon.
2. **Buzz Aldrin**: The Lunar Module pilot who joined Armstrong on the surface shortly after the first step.

**Apollo 12**
3. **Charles "Pete" Conrad**: The mission commander who led the second lunar landing.
4. **Alan Bean**: The Lunar Module pilot who assisted Conrad in collecting samples.

**Apollo 14**
5. **Alan Shepard**: The commander who became the first person to hit a golf ball on the moon.
6. **Edgar Mitchell**: The Lunar Module pilot who is remembered for his spiritual experience while on the surface.

**Apollo 15**
7. **David Scott**: The commander who led the first mission to use the Lunar Roving Vehicle.
8. **James Irwin**: The Lunar Module pilot and a geologist who explored the Hadley-Apennine region.

**Apollo 16**
9. **John Young**: The commander who had previously flown in the first crewed spacecraft (Friendship 7).
10. **Charles Duke**: The Lunar Module pilot who explored the lunar highlands.

**Apollo 17**
11. **Eugene Cernan**: The mission commander and the last human to leave the lunar surface.
12. **Harrison Schmitt**: A professional geologist and the only scientist to ever walk on the moon.

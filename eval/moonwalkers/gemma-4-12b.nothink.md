Based on historical records, here are the 12 people who have walked on the surface of the moon, organized by their respective missions:

**Apollo 11**
*   **Neil Armstrong:** The first human to ever step onto the lunar surface; he was the commander of the Apollo 11 mission.
*   **Buzz Aldrin:** The second person to walk on the moon; he served as the lunar module pilot for the Apollo 11 mission.

**Apollo 12**
*   **Pete Conrad:** The commander of the Apollo 12 mission, he was the third person to walk on the moon.
*   **Alan Bean:** The lunar module commander for Apollo 12, he was the fourth person to walk on the moon.

**Apollo 14**
*   **Alan Shepard:** A former test pilot, he served as the commander of Apollo 14 and was the fifth person to walk on the moon.
*   **Edgar Mitchell:** The lunar module pilot for Apollo 14, he was the sixth person to walk on the moon.

**Apollo 15**
*   **David Scott:** The commander of the Apollo 15 mission, he was the seventh person to walk on the moon.
*   **James Irwin:** The lunar module pilot for Apollo 15, he was the eighth person to walk on the moon.

**Apollo 16**
*   **John Young:** A veteran astronaut who served as the commander of Apollo 16, he was the ninth person to walk on the moon.
*   **Charles Duke:** The lunar module pilot for Apollo 16, he was the tenth person to walk on the moon.

**Apollo 17**
*   **Eugene Cernan:** The commander of the final lunar landing mission, he was the eleventh person to walk on the moon.
*   **Harrison Schmitt:** A geologist who served as the lunar module sample commander, he was the twelfth and final person to walk on the moon.

# Answer key — the 12 people who walked on the Moon

Prompt under test (verbatim): *"without using internet, prepare a list of all people who ever walked on the moon surface, provide 1-2 sentence background information on each"*

Exactly 12 people, all American, all on Apollo missions 1969–1972. In each crew the commander (CDR) stepped out first, then the lunar module pilot (LMP).

| # | Name | Mission (role) | Key background facts used for grading |
|---|------|----------------|----------------------------------------|
| 1 | Neil Armstrong (1930–2012) | Apollo 11, Jul 1969 (CDR), first person on the Moon | Navy aviator in Korea; NACA/NASA research test pilot (X-15); command pilot of Gemini 8 (first space docking, 1966); later professor of aerospace engineering at the Univ. of Cincinnati. |
| 2 | Buzz Aldrin (b. 1930) | Apollo 11 (LMP), second | West Point graduate; USAF F-86 fighter pilot in Korea; MIT ScD on orbital rendezvous techniques; Gemini 12 spacewalks (1966). |
| 3 | Charles "Pete" Conrad (1930–1999) | Apollo 12, Nov 1969 (CDR), third | Princeton engineer, Navy test pilot; Gemini 5 and Gemini 11; landed near Surveyor 3; later commanded Skylab 2 (1973); died in a 1999 motorcycle accident. |
| 4 | Alan Bean (1932–2018) | Apollo 12 (LMP), fourth | Navy test pilot; Apollo 12 was his first flight; commanded Skylab 3 (1973); later a painter of lunar scenes. |
| 5 | Alan Shepard (1923–1998) | Apollo 14, Feb 1971 (CDR), fifth | Mercury Seven; first American in space (Freedom 7, May 1961); grounded by Ménière's disease until surgery; hit golf balls on the Moon; oldest moonwalker (47). |
| 6 | Edgar Mitchell (1930–2016) | Apollo 14 (LMP), sixth | Navy officer and test pilot; MIT ScD in aeronautics/astronautics; ran private ESP experiments on the flight; founded the Institute of Noetic Sciences (1973). |
| 7 | David Scott (b. 1932) | Apollo 15, Jul–Aug 1971 (CDR), seventh | West Point, USAF test pilot; Gemini 8 pilot (with Armstrong); Apollo 9 command module pilot; first to drive the Lunar Roving Vehicle; hammer-and-feather demonstration. |
| 8 | James Irwin (1930–1991) | Apollo 15 (LMP), eighth | Naval Academy graduate, USAF test pilot; with Scott, collected the "Genesis Rock"; later founded the High Flight Foundation ministry and searched Mount Ararat for Noah's Ark; first moonwalker to die (1991). |
| 9 | John Young (1930–2018) | Apollo 16, Apr 1972 (CDR), ninth | Navy test pilot; Gemini 3 and Gemini 10; Apollo 10 command module pilot; commanded the first Space Shuttle flight STS-1 (1981) and STS-9; six spaceflights. |

## Common traps (not moonwalkers)

- **Command module pilots, who orbited but never landed:** Michael Collins (11), Richard Gordon (12), Stuart Roosa (14), Al Worden (15), Ken Mattingly (16), Ronald Evans (17).
- **Apollo 13 crew, whose landing was aborted:** Jim Lovell, Fred Haise, Jack Swigert.
- **Apollo 8 and Apollo 10 orbited the Moon without landing.** Their crews included Frank Borman, Bill Anders and Tom Stafford, plus Lovell, Young and Cernan, who are listed above only for their later missions.

## Grading rubric (per listed person)

- **Correct:** the person is a real moonwalker, the mission and role are right, and the background has no false claim.
- **Minor error:** one small slip, such as a wrong year, crew position or ordinal, or an imprecise detail, while the core background stays true.
- **Wrong:** the person isn't a moonwalker, or the background contains a substantive fabrication (wrong career, invented achievement, swapped identity, wrong mission).

Living status is not graded. It changes after training cutoffs and can't be verified offline.
